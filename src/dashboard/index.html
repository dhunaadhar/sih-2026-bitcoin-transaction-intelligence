Investigative-use limitation

Risk scores, anomaly signals, behavioral patterns, graph relationships, network evidence and model explanations are designed to prioritize investigation. They do not establish real-world identity, ownership, intent, illicit activity or guilt.

The system operates on pseudonymous blockchain and network evidence and should be interpreted together with independent investigative evidence.
